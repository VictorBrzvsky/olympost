Inside step 4

The autopilot pipeline

The platform scans 20–100 competitor accounts in your niche, breaks their videos down into hooks, expressions, delivery and editing pace, builds a content plan, writes scripts, generates the videos a day ahead of schedule and queues them for publishing.

  6. 4.6Queue for publishing on schedule

FAQ

Questions before the call

Not here? Ask on the call.

Book a call
Do we need to shoot anything?

No. Once, you send photos or a video, a voice sample, a product description, geo, languages and stop-words. The character and every video are created on the platform from there.

How do we test it before buying?

Book a call and we open test access without the admin panel. You make 3 videos on your product, in your language, with your character, before any payment.

What if we do not like the result?

Three things are fixed. You see 3 videos before you pay. If the platform restricts an account for technical reasons, we replace it, as the contract states. If we do not deliver the promised volume in the first month, you get a pro-rated refund.

Can we approve videos before they are published?

Yes. Approve every video by hand or switch to full auto. You can change the mode at any time.

How many videos a day can one account publish?

Recommended 1–3 per account per day; the technical ceiling is 20. Plans are sized at one video a day per account: 50 accounts on Managed, 120 on Growth, 250 on Network. Above the plan, an extra account is $35 a month with one video a day included.

What does "unique" mean?

A unique video is generated from scratch. Each one is then re-cut into four variants for different accounts. One unique video becomes five posts.

What about bans?

A self-learning AI analyses the cause of every restriction and adjusts the behaviour of the other accounts on the fly, so the same mistake does not repeat. If the platform restricts an account for technical reasons, we replace it under the contract. We reduce technical risk, but we do not override the rules of social networks: content that breaks them gets restricted on any platform.

Why are videos cheaper than on AI generation platforms?

Generation at wholesale rates, and every unique video turns into five posts. So a published video costs $1.33 on Managed and $0.88 on Network, while AI video platforms charge $1.50–2.75 for a 15-second video.

What does each plan include, and what happens after a year?

License ($12,000) is the platform, character, pipeline, prompts, training and a year of updates; you run it on your own accounts and pay for infrastructure directly. Managed ($24,000) adds full account management: 50 accounts, 1,500 videos a month and a monthly report. Growth ($45,000) raises that to 120 accounts and 3,600 videos with a dedicated account manager. Network ($79,000) is 250 accounts and 7,500 videos, with priority generation, a quarterly strategy session and white-label rights.

Renewal costs less because onboarding does not repeat: $10,000 for License updates and support, $22,000 for Managed, $41,000 for Growth and $72,000 for Network. If you do not renew a License, your character and settings stay with you; updates and support stop.

Why is infrastructure paid separately on License?

Because it depends on your volume. On License you run the platform on your own accounts and pay the providers directly for what you use. On Managed and above, everything is included in the plan price.

What if we need more volume?

Overage is published above. Or move up a plan — the price per account drops with every tier: $40 a month on Managed, $31.25 on Growth, $26.33 on Network. If none of the plans fits, we put together individual terms.

Can I resell it?

Yes, on Network. See white-label above.

Which social networks do you work with?

Instagram is the core: plans count Instagram accounts, from setup to daily posting. We also post to TikTok and YouTube: $4 and $7 per account a month on top of the plan. For a different platform mix, we put together individual terms.
