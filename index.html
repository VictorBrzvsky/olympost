Do we need to shoot anything?

No. Once, you send photos or a video, a voice sample, a product description, geo, languages and stop-words. The character and every video are created on the platform from there.

How many videos a day can one account publish?

As many as you need. We recommend 1–3 a day per account, so the accounts stay safe. The number of accounts has no limit.

What does "unique" mean?

A unique video is generated from scratch, then re-cut into variants for different accounts. How many of your videos are unique, we set on the call.

Whose accounts are they?

We create and run the accounts on our own phones, so you do not buy or set up anything. If an account gets blocked, we replace it right away. If you leave, we hand the accounts over to you.
