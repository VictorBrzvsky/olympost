Still deciding?

Do we need to shoot anything?

No. Once, you send photos or a video, a voice sample, a product description, geo, languages and stop-words. The character and every video are created on the platform from there.

How many videos a day can one account publish?

As many as you need. We recommend 1–3 per account per day. Plans are sized at one video a day per account: 50 accounts on Managed, 120 on Growth, 250 on Network.

What does "unique" mean?

A unique video is generated from scratch, then re-cut into four variants for different accounts. One unique video becomes five posts. On Managed, 300 of the 1,500 videos a month are unique.

What if it is not for us?

You have 21 days after payment. Ask for a refund and you get the full amount back.

What happens after a year?

Renewal costs less because onboarding does not repeat: $10,000 for License, $22,000 for Managed, $41,000 for Growth and $72,000 for Network. If you do not renew a License, your character and settings stay with you; updates and support stop.

What if we need more volume?

Above the plan, an extra account is $35 a month with one video a day included. Or move up a plan. If none of them fits, we put together individual terms.

Which social networks do you work with?

Instagram is the core: plans count Instagram accounts, from setup to daily posting. We also post to TikTok and YouTube, at $4 and $7 per account a month on top of the plan.
