Still deciding?

Do we need to shoot anything?

No. Once, you send photos or a video, a voice sample, a product description, geo, languages and stop-words. The character and every video are created on the platform from there.

How many videos a day can one account publish?

As many as you need. We recommend 1–3 per account per day. Annual plans are sized at one video a day per account: 50 accounts on Managed, 120 on Growth, 250 on Network. Prime is sized at 3 a day on 30 accounts.

What does "unique" mean?

A unique video is generated from scratch, then re-cut into four variants for different accounts. One unique video becomes five posts. On Managed, 3,600 of the 18,000 videos a year are unique. On Prime, all 2,700 a month are unique.

Can we start without a year?

Yes. Prime is paid month to month: $6,000 for 2,700 unique videos on 30 accounts, with no annual contract. If you move to an annual plan later, the Prime month is not credited toward its price.

How long does launch take?

3–7 days on every plan. In that time the character, the accounts and the content plan are created.

What if it is not for us?

On annual plans you have 21 days after payment. Ask for a refund and you get the full amount back. On Prime the refund window is 7 days.

Why do you name so few clients?

We cannot disclose everyone we work with. The names on this page are the ones we can share. Ask on the call what we can show for your niche.

What happens after a year?

Renewal costs less because onboarding does not repeat: $12,500 for License, $27,500 for Managed, $51,000 for Growth and $90,000 for Network. If you do not renew a License, your character and settings stay with you; updates and support stop.

What if we need more volume?

Above the plan, an extra account is $45 a month with one video a day included. Or move up a plan. If none of them fits, we put together individual terms.

Which social networks do you work with?

Instagram is the core: plans count Instagram accounts, from setup to daily posting. The platform also posts to TikTok and YouTube, at $4 and $7 per account a month on top of the plan.
